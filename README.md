# Blockchain-Based Supply Chain Visibility Compliance Monitoring

A comprehensive blockchain solution for monitoring, tracking, and ensuring compliance across supply chain operations. This system provides transparent, immutable, and automated compliance management for modern supply chains.

## Overview

This system leverages blockchain technology to create a transparent and auditable supply chain compliance framework. It enables organizations to monitor compliance standards, detect violations, manage corrective actions, and maintain certifications across their entire supply chain network.

## Key Features

### 🔍 Real-Time Compliance Monitoring
- Continuous monitoring of supply chain activities
- Automated compliance verification processes
- Real-time alerts and notifications for compliance issues

### 📊 Standards Tracking
- Comprehensive tracking of industry standards and regulations
- Version control for evolving compliance requirements
- Multi-standard support (ISO, FDA, HACCP, etc.)

### 🚨 Violation Detection
- Automated detection of compliance violations
- Risk assessment and severity classification
- Historical violation tracking and analysis

### 🔧 Corrective Action Management
- Structured corrective action workflows
- Progress tracking and deadline management
- Stakeholder notification and collaboration tools

### 🏆 Certification Management
- Digital certificate issuance and verification
- Expiration tracking and renewal reminders
- Multi-party certification validation

## System Architecture

The system is built on a modular architecture with five core components:

1. **Compliance Monitor Verification**: Validates and manages compliance monitoring entities
2. **Standards Tracking**: Maintains and tracks compliance standards and requirements
3. **Violation Detection**: Identifies and categorizes compliance violations
4. **Corrective Action Management**: Orchestrates remediation workflows
5. **Certification Management**: Handles digital certifications and validations
